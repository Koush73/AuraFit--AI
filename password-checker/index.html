About SecureGuard

How scoring works and what we check.

🔒

Scoring Methodology

Passwords are scored on a 1–10 scale across four dimensions: Length (3pts), Complexity (3pts), Uniqueness/Entropy (2pts), and Pattern avoidance (2pts). Scores are combined and rounded to one decimal place.

📊

Grade Thresholds

  • Weak – 1 to 3.9
  • Fair – 4 to 5.9
  • Good – 6 to 7.9
  • Strong – 8 to 9.4
  • Very Strong – 9.5 to 10
🛡️

Privacy First

All analysis happens 100% client-side in your browser. No password data is ever sent to a server. Your history is stored locally in localStorage under your username.

📁

CSV Export

Export your entire password history as a CSV file including: timestamp, masked password, score, grade, and all individual criteria flags. Use this to audit your password hygiene.
